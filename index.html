Recent launches.

A snapshot of 2025–2026 builds. Each one shipped with bespoke motion, custom design, and zero templates.

Lumen OS

SaaS dashboard · Design + build · 2026

The Vision

Lumen OS needed a radical redesign of their dense analytics platform. The goal was to transform a utilitarian data dashboard into a premium, cinematic experience that felt like a luxury tool.

The Build

We engineered a highly responsive React 19 interface with perfect dark mode contrast, zero-latency transitions, and a dense, architectural layout that prioritizes keyboard-first interaction.

The Outcome

Post-launch, user session lengths increased by 42%, and the aesthetic overhaul directly contributed to their successful Series B raise. It now sets the industry standard for SaaS UI.

Halo Studios

Agency site · 3D hero · 2025

The Vision

Halo Studios, a premier creative agency, wanted a portfolio that broke away from standard grid templates. They needed a site that felt like an immersive, interactive art gallery.

The Build

We built a custom WebGL hero sequence using Three.js, integrated with a seamless, scroll-linked cinematic animation system. Every interaction was designed to feel heavy and deliberate.

The Outcome

The site won Site of the Month on Awwwards, dropping bounce rates by 28% and elevating the agency's perceived value to match their elite enterprise clientele.

Nova Skincare

DTC launch · WebGL bottle · 2025

The Vision

Nova Skincare was launching a luxury DTC product and needed their digital storefront to reflect the physical quality of their frosted glass packaging.

The Build

We implemented a real-time WebGL interactive 3D bottle that users could spin and inspect, wrapped in an ultra-minimalist, high-fashion monochrome layout.

The Outcome

The immersive 3D experience led to a 15% higher conversion rate compared to standard static imagery, positioning Nova as a top-tier luxury brand from day one.

Driftly AI

YC startup · Marketing site · 2025

The Vision

Driftly AI needed to stand out in a sea of generic AI startups. They requested a "calm" approach to AI—avoiding the typical glowing neon tropes.

The Build

We stripped away all color, focusing purely on typographic hierarchy, smooth GSAP motion trails, and deep shadows, delivering a site that felt like an editorial magazine.

The Outcome

The launch was highly successful, attracting immediate attention from tier-1 VCs who noted the "incredibly mature" visual presence for a seed-stage company.
